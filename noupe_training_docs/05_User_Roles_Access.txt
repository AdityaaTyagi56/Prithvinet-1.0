PrithviNet: User Roles & Access Hierarchies

The platform is designed with a secure role-based login system that supports multiple distinct personas, each with unique abilities and dashboard perspectives.

1. Super Admin:
- Has absolute control over the platform.
- Responsible for Entity and Master Management.
- On-boards Regional Offices, Industries, Water Sources, and Monitoring teams.
- Configures monitoring locations with geo-tagging, monitoring units (dB, ppm, °C), and sets the "prescribed limits" for every environmental parameter.

2. Regional Officer:
- Observes the macro-level environmental health of their designated jurisdiction.
- Approves special monitoring campaigns.
- Reviews compliance statuses and handles escalation workflows from industries exceeding prescribed limits.

3. Monitoring Team:
- Ground-level staff that interact with sensor setups.
- Conduct special campaigns and upload specific air, water, and noise data submissions.
- Troubleshoots anomalous readings.

4. Industry User:
- Connects their industrial monitoring logs to the platform.
- Views their internal compliance scorecard to avoid penalization.
- Receives automated missing report reminders and warning alerts when their exhaust approaches prescribed thresholds.

5. Citizen:
- Engages with the "Citizen Transparency Portal".
- Views real-time monitoring dashboards, public datasets, geo-spatial pollution heatmaps, and simplified comparison charts. 
- Goal is to promote citizen-awareness without exposing granular proprietary industrial models.
