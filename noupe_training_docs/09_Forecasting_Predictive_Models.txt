PrithviNet: Forecasting and Predictive Models

An integral part of the Innovation Challenge module is projecting the immediate environmental future.

Multi-step Forecasting Scope: 
- Designed to predict environmental parameters strictly for the next 24 to 72 hours.
- Used to foresee bad air/water events before the citizen population is directly harmed.

Output Requirements:
1. Point Forecasts:
- Exact predicted values (e.g., "At 14:00 tomorrow, PM2.5 will hit 115 ug/m3").
2. Uncertainty Estimates:
- Statistical confidence intervals (e.g., "± 10 ug/m3" or "85% confidence").

Methodologies embedded in the architecture:
- Time-series modeling based on the historical logs stored in `backend/data/aqi_logs/`.
- Factors in diurnal patterns (the natural up-and-down of temperature/sunlight/traffic throughout a 24-hour cycle).
- Displayed natively in the Web Interface within Comparison Charts and Trend Analysis panels.
