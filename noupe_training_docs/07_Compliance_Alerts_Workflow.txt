PrithviNet: Alerts and Compliance Workflow

The old siloed approach leads to non-compliance by industries and delayed reporting. PrithviNet revolutionizes this via automated thresholding.

Prescribed Limits:
- Limits are configured at an administrative level (e.g., PM2.5 > 60 is flagged, PM10 > 100 is flagged).

Alert Types:
1. Limit Exceedance Alerts:
- Triggered automatically via backend state checks when an IoT sensor or API hook pushes reading data higher than the designated upper bounds.
- System shifts statuses from green (Safe) to red (Hazardous).

2. Missing Report Reminders:
- Tracks when industries or local stations fail to ping the database within their expected temporal window. Automated reminders are sent.

Tracking & Response:
- Non-compliance Tracking Dashboard: Allows Regional Officers to view a ranked list of the worst local offenders.
- Escalation Workflow to Authorities: Repeated infractions lead to a logged "Incident" and trigger email/notification escalations to state authorities to apply consequences or dispatch a manual monitoring team.
