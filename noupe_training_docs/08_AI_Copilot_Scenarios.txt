PrithviNet: AI Assisted Compliance Copilot

PrithviNet embeds a conversational AI agent designed specifically for advanced queries regarding environment interventions. As a requirement of PS01, Noupe (the chatbot) must understand Structural Models / Causal Graphs combining emissions, meteorology, and pollution data, as well as Learned Surrogate models.

Core Capabilities the AI must handle:
- Natural Language querying over CSV/Database statistics.
- Hypothetical Interventions (Counterfactuals).

Primary Scenarios outlined in CECB PS01:
Scenario 1: Emission Adjustments
Query: "If industry X reduces emissions of pollutant Y by 30 percent, what is the expected change in regional risk over the next week?"
Expected Logic: The AI correlates Industry X's current output levels, extrapolates a 30% cut, references the current predictive 7-day model for the local geo-tagged region, and provides an estimate (e.g., "The AQI will likely drop from 180 to 145 over 7 days").

Scenario 2: Festival / Periodic Shutdowns
Query: "What happens if the top high risk units are temporarily shut down during a festival period?"
Expected Logic: The AI identifies the "Top High Risk Units" in the database by highest average exceedance, simulates a 100% emission reduction from those coordinates for the specific festival days, and presents the delta. 

The AI should act strictly as an "AI driven compliance assistant" and anomaly detector, relying heavily on existing trends and the 24-72 hour forecasts.
